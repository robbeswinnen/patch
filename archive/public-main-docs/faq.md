# FAQ

==- Patch cannot find a player. What should I do?
Try the player ID first. Names can change or collide, but an ID is much easier for Patch to follow.
==-

==- Why does `/stats` have pages?
The overview page stays readable, and the menu lets you open deeper season and history pages only when you want them.
==-

==- Why is `/track` private?
Your watchlist is yours. Patch replies privately and sends recaps by DM.
==-

==- Why do reports need proof?
Staff needs something concrete to review. A clear image or video helps everyone move faster and keeps the process fair.
==-

==- Can a report be removed?
Yes, but only through the developer-only tools. That keeps public status changes intentional.
==-

==- Does Patch decide who is guilty?
No. Patch organizes public data and sends reports to staff. Staff makes the review decision.
==-

==- What does `Secure` mean?
It means Patch has no accepted community report or curated tag for that player. It is a clean public read, not a magic shield.
==-
